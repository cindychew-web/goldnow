Questions

Everything you ask before you start

Still unsure? Talk to the team.

What is GoldNow?

GoldNow is a savings app by Tomei Group that lets you buy, save, and redeem real 999.9 purity gold in small amounts, split across multiple goal-based pots.

How is GoldNow different from buying physical gold?

You save in any amount, anytime, with no storage risk. Your gold is fully allocated and insured in Tomei's vaults, and you can redeem or withdraw instantly from the app instead of visiting a store.

Is my gold real and insured?

Yes. Every gram is certified 999.9 purity, fully allocated to you (never pooled or leveraged), and held in audited, insured vaults.

Can I withdraw or redeem my gold anytime?

Yes, you can withdraw or redeem from any pot anytime directly in the app — most redemptions complete in under two minutes.

How fast can I start saving?

Download the app, verify your identity once, link a debit or credit card, and you can create your first savings pot in minutes.
