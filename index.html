FAQ Before you register
Who is eligible for the gold reward? +

Any car insurance policy renewed through A to Z Risk Management with a sum insured of RM100,000 or above. Coverage type and vehicle model don't affect eligibility.

How much gold do I receive? +

The current campaign credits 0.1g of gold per eligible renewal to your GoldNow account. Exact amounts are confirmed at the point of renewal and may vary by promotion.

When does the gold arrive? +

Gold is credited to your GoldNow account once your renewed policy is confirmed by A to Z Risk Management, typically within a few business days.

Do I need a GoldNow account first? +

No. Register your interest below and, if you don't already have GoldNow, our team will help you set one up so the gold has somewhere to land.

Is there any extra cost? +

No. The gold reward is on top of your normal insurance premium — there's no additional fee to participate.
