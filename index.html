What's the minimum amount I can buy?

You can start saving with as little as 0.1g — no large lump sums needed, so you can begin right away and top up whenever it suits you.

What's the maximum duration I can set for my goal?

You can set a savings timeline of up to 120 months (10 years) for any pot, whatever fits your goal.

How fast can I start saving?

Download the app, verify your identity once, link a debit or credit card, and you can create your first savings pot in minutes.

How do I redeem gold or cash out?

You can redeem physical gold at any Tomei outlet nationwide, starting from as low as 1g. Or cash out straight to your bank account from within the app, starting from as low as 0.1g.
